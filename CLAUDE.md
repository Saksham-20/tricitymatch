## QA method notes (web, Playwright)
- **Static full-page screenshots LIE about scroll-animated content.** `react-countup` (`enableScrollSpy`) renders at start value (**0**) and `framer-motion whileInView` stays `opacity:0` until in view → a `fullPage` shot shows "0+/0K+" stats and "blank" sections that are **artifacts, not bugs**. Scroll the element into view (`window.scrollTo`) THEN screenshot before flagging.
- Onboarding/Login render the **global Navbar** (sticky) — in `fullPage` shots it can overlap mid-page; viewport-level shots are truer.
- Dev OTP e2e: local `OTP_BYPASS_CODES` is **unset** by default (prod-only per memory) + SMS is live (real texts). To drive a full verify e2e, set `OTP_BYPASS_CODES=000000` in `.env.development`, **touch `backend/server.js`** to make nodemon reload (it does NOT auto-restart on `.env` change, and an external `kill` of its child does not respawn it), test, then revert. Don't send real SMS to arbitrary numbers.
